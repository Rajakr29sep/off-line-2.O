• Drop down to your knees and make yourself small, cover your head and neck, and protect your face [1]
• If indoors, practice drop, cover, hold until the shaking stops and you are sure it is safe to exit [1]
• If you are travelling in a vehicle, pull off the road to a clear area, stop the vehicle, and apply parking brake [1]
• If you are near an exit point, quickly move outside and stand in open space [1]
• Cover your head and neck with one arm and hand, and hold on to a sturdy table or desk for shelter [2]

=== What precautions should I take during a flood?

* Seal entry points around doors and vents, and seal windows if water is likely to rise that high [1]
* Do not drive in flood water as it can wash away even big vehicles [2]
* Stay away from electric poles and fallen power lines to avoid electrocution [2]
* Wash hands with soap and water before eating food or feeding young ones [2]
* Turn off the main power supply [1]
